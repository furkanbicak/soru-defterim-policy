Privacy Policy for Soru Defterim

Effective date: November 21, 2026

Türkçe · Kullanım Koşulları · KVKK Aydınlatma Metni

Soru Defterim ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Ahmet Furkan Bıçak, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app processes, how that information is used, and the choices available to you when using the app.

1. Information We Process

Soru Defterim is a study app that helps students save questions they got wrong and review them on a spaced-repetition schedule until their exam.

Depending on how you use the app, the following information may be processed:

  • Question and solution photos you take or select, and the subject, topic, error reasons, and notes you add
  • Your review history, review schedule, practice-exam results, exam type, exam date, and reminder settings
  • When you use an AI feature: the photo of that question, your own solution photo if present, and its exam, subject, and topic
  • A random installation identifier and, if you purchase Premium, an anonymous purchase identifier, as explained in Sections 3 and 4

2. How Your Data Is Stored

Your questions, photos, notes, review history, and settings are stored locally on your device, in the app's private storage. No user account is required.

Photos are copied into the app; the originals in your photo library are not modified or uploaded. We cannot remotely access the records on your device.

3. AI Features and OpenAI

Soru Defterim offers optional AI features: "Solve with AI" (step-by-step explanation), "Why did I get it wrong?" (finds the mistake in your own solution), and "Generate similar questions".

When you use one of these features, the app sends over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our server at api.foxvix.online:

  • the photo of that question, and your own solution photo if you added one
  • the exam, subject, and topic of that question
  • a random installation identifier, used to apply the free usage limit and prevent abuse
  • if you purchased Premium, an anonymous purchase identifier, used to verify Premium

Our server forwards the content to our AI provider, OpenAI (United States), only to generate the response. We do not keep the photos after the response is generated. Under OpenAI's API policies, data sent through the API is not used to train its models and may be retained for a limited period for abuse monitoring.

These transfers happen only after you give explicit consent the first time you use an AI feature. You can withdraw consent at any time in Ayarlar → Yasal ve destek → Yapay Zekâ Veri Paylaşımı (Settings → Legal and support → AI data sharing). Everything except the AI features keeps working without consent.

AI responses may be inaccurate. You can report an inappropriate or incorrect response with the "Bu yanıtı bildir" (Report this response) link on the response screen.

  • https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy

4. Purchases and RevenueCat

Premium is a one-time, non-renewing in-app purchase made through Google Play or the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

The validity of your purchase is verified through RevenueCat, Inc. (United States). RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt and an anonymous app user identifier; it does not receive your questions, photos, or notes.

  • https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

5. How We Use Information

We use processed information to:

  • provide the core functionality of the app: saving questions and scheduling reviews
  • generate AI explanations, mistake analyses, and similar questions when you request them
  • apply the free AI usage limit and prevent abuse
  • verify and restore Premium purchases
  • schedule local daily review reminders when you grant notification permission
  • create PDF worksheets on your device when you request them

Reminders and PDFs are created locally on your device; no remote notification server is used.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

Your data is shared only with the service providers described above (OpenAI for AI features you request, RevenueCat for purchase verification) and only for those purposes. When you share a PDF worksheet, you choose where it goes, and that destination processes it under its own terms.

7. Tracking and Advertising

Soru Defterim does not show ads. The app does not use an advertising identifier, does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

8. Permissions

  • Camera: to photograph questions and your solutions
  • Photos: to pick a question image from your library (only the image you select)
  • Notifications: for daily review reminders

You can turn these permissions off at any time in your device settings.

9. Data Retention

App records remain on your device until you delete them, use Ayarlar → Tüm Verileri Sil (Settings → Delete all data), or uninstall the app.

Photos sent for AI features are not stored by us after the response is generated. The installation identifier and usage counters are kept only as long as needed to apply usage limits. Third-party providers may retain technical records under their own policies.

10. Your Choices and Rights

You can:

  • edit or delete any question, note, or record inside the app
  • delete all app data using Ayarlar → Tüm Verileri Sil (Settings → Delete all data)
  • withdraw consent for AI data sharing in the app settings
  • manage camera, photo, and notification permissions in your device settings
  • uninstall the app to remove its data from your device

If you are in Türkiye, you have the rights described in Article 11 of the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). See the KVKK Aydınlatma Metni. You can exercise your rights by contacting us at the address in Section 14.

11. Children's Privacy

Soru Defterim is intended for students aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Users under 18 should talk to a parent or guardian before using the AI features.

12. Security

Data sent to our server is transmitted over HTTPS. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your device protected, and photograph only the question itself so that photos do not contain personal information.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published at this address with a revised effective date.

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. No advertising, analytics scripts, or cookies have been added to the page. GitHub may process technical visitor information, such as IP addresses, to serve the page under its own Privacy Statement:

  • https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
